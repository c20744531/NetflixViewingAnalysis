How I did it

Python, from raw export to this page
  • Data: my Netflix viewing history export (Account, Profile, Viewing activity, Download all). Each row is a title and the date I watched it.
  • Cleaning: parsed the dates and split titles like "The Night Agent: Season 3: Orion" into the show name and episode.
  • Tagging: hand-tagged all 73 shows and films with a genre, film or series, and country of production in a lookup table, then joined it to the history.
  • Analysis: monthly and weekday counts, top series, binge sessions (3+ episodes of one show in a day) and country and genre splits.
  • Watch time: Netflix doesn't export durations, so hours are an estimate at 45 minutes per episode and 110 per film.
  • Visuals: charts drawn as SVG in plain JavaScript; the world map is pre-rendered with d3-geo and Natural Earth data.
